Two routines

Morning vs evening

Morning routine

3 steps · 2 minutes

    Cleanse

    Gentle rinse — no need for a full cleanse if you cleansed last night.

  2. 2
    Treat

Evening routine

4 steps · 3 minutes

  1. 1
    Double cleanse

    Remove makeup and SPF thoroughly. Lukewarm water.

  2. 2
    Tone (optional)

    Calming toner to rebalance skin after cleansing.

  3. 3
    Treat

    Retinol 2–3 nights a week, hydrating serum on off-nights.

  4. 4
    Moisturise

    Richer moisturiser or overnight mask for repair.

Questions

Common questions

How long until I see results?

Hydration improves within days. Texture and tone take 4–6 weeks of consistent use. Fine lines and dark spots can take 8–12 weeks.

Can I use retinol and vitamin C together?

Yes — but not at the same time. Vitamin C in the morning, retinol at night. Never layer them.

Do I really need SPF every day?

Yes. UV damage is the number one cause of premature ageing. Even on cloudy days, up to 80% of UV rays pass through.

What if my skin reacts?

Stop the new product and return to your basics for a week. If irritation continues, contact us on WhatsApp — we'll help.

Can I start with just 2 products?

Absolutely. Start with a cleanser and moisturiser. Add SPF next, then serums. Build the routine as your skin adjusts.

Which moisturiser is right for me?

Oily skin: lightweight gel. Dry skin: richer cream. Combination: our Barrier Repair Moisturiser works for almost everyone.
